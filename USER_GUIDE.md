## Getting Started

### What is RupeeMap?

RupeeMap is a comprehensive financial planning tool with 58+ calculators, an AI-powered chatbot, and a financial literacy platform — all designed with Indian financial context (₹, PPF, NPS, ELSS, Nifty 50, SEBI regulations).

### System Requirements

- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Internet connection (for AI chatbot and stock data)
- No installation required — runs in your browser

### First Visit

When you first open RupeeMap, you'll see:
- **Left sidebar** — Navigation with all calculators organized by category
- **Main area** — Landing page with quick access to popular calculators
- **ArthaAI button** — Top of sidebar for instant AI help

## Retirement Calculators

### Deterministic Calculator
**Best for:** Simple retirement planning with fixed assumptions

Enter your current age, retirement age, life expectancy, monthly expenses, inflation rate, and expected returns. The calculator shows:
- Required retirement corpus
- Monthly investment needed
- Break-even withdrawal rate
- Year-by-year projection table

**How to use:**
1. Enter your current age and when you want to retire
2. Input your current monthly expenses
3. Set expected inflation (typically 6-7% in India)
4. Set expected returns (equity: 12%, debt: 7%)
5. Click Calculate

### Stochastic (Monte Carlo) Calculator
**Best for:** Understanding range of possible outcomes with market volatility

Runs 1,000 simulations using historical market data to show:
- Success probability (% of simulations where money lasts)
- Best case, median, and worst case outcomes
- Portfolio value over time chart with confidence bands

**Key insight:** Unlike the deterministic calculator, this accounts for market ups and downs — showing you the probability of running out of money.

### What-if Calculator
**Best for:** Testing different scenarios and "what happens if" questions

Features 10,000 Monte Carlo simulations with:
- Percentile bands (P10, P25, P50, P75, P90)
- Interactive area chart showing possible outcomes
- Break-even withdrawal rate calculation

**Try these scenarios:**
- What if I retire 5 years earlier?
- What if inflation is higher than expected?
- What if I increase my savings by ₹10,000/month?

### Test Adequacy Calculator
**Best for:** Checking if your current corpus is sufficient

Enter your existing retirement corpus and see:
- Probability of the money lasting your lifetime
- Recommended withdrawal amount
- Risk analysis across market conditions

### Withdrawal Rates Calculator
**Best for:** Finding safe withdrawal rates for retirement

Analyzes different withdrawal rates (3%, 4%, 5%, etc.) using historical data to show:
- Success rate for each withdrawal rate
- Optimal withdrawal strategy
- Historical performance across different time periods

### History Back-test Calculator
**Best for:** Seeing how retirement would have worked in different eras

Uses actual historical market returns (1979-2025) to show:
- How different starting years performed
- Impact of market crashes (1992, 2000, 2008, 2020)
- Best and worst historical scenarios

## Investment Calculators

### SIP Calculator
**Best for:** Planning Systematic Investment Plans

Enter monthly investment, expected return rate, and duration to see:
- Total amount invested
- Wealth gained through compounding
- Maturity value

**Example:** ₹5,000/month for 20 years at 12% returns = ₹49.96 lakhs (from ₹12 lakhs invested)

### Lumpsum Calculator
**Best for:** One-time investment planning

Enter a single investment amount, expected return rate, and duration to see future value.

### Step-up SIP Calculator
**Best for:** Planning increasing SIP amounts

Increase your SIP annually (e.g., 10% yearly) to see how small annual increases dramatically boost returns over time.

### SWP Calculator (Systematic Withdrawal Plan)
**Best for:** Planning regular withdrawals from investments

Enter your corpus, monthly withdrawal needed, and expected returns to see how long your money lasts.

### STP Calculator (Systematic Transfer Plan)
**Best for:** Transferring between mutual funds systematically

Plan regular transfers from debt to equity funds (or vice versa).

### Goal Planner
**Best for:** Saving for specific financial goals

Enter your goal (house, education, marriage), target amount, time horizon, and current savings to see:
- Monthly investment needed
- Shortfall analysis
- Recommended fund allocation

### CAGR Calculator
**Best for:** Calculating compound annual growth rate

Enter start value, end value, and duration to find the actual annualized return on your investment.

### XIRR Calculator
**Best for:** Calculating returns on irregular cash flows

Input multiple transactions with dates (investments and redemptions) to find the actual return rate — more accurate than CAGR for real-world investing.

### Returns Calculator
**Best for:** Comparing different investment scenarios

Compare multiple investment options side by side with different return assumptions.

### Stock Price Lookup
**Best for:** Quick stock price checks

Search any Indian stock (NSE/BSE) by name or symbol to see:
- Current price
- Day high/low
- 52-week high/low
- Market cap
- Volume

## Savings Calculators

### FD Calculator
**Best for:** Fixed Deposit planning

Enter deposit amount, interest rate, and tenure to see maturity value. Compare across banks (6-8% typical rates).

### RD Calculator
**Best for:** Recurring Deposit planning

Calculate maturity value for monthly deposits. Great for building discipline.

### PPF Calculator
**Best for:** Public Provident Fund (15-year lock-in)

Features:
- Annual contribution limit: ₹1.5 lakh
- Current rate: 7.1% (compounded annually)
- Tax-free maturity under EEE category

### EPF Calculator
**Best for:** Employee Provident Fund

Calculate employee + employer contribution and interest accumulation.

### NPS Calculator
**Best for:** National Pension System

Calculate corpus and annuity under NPS with:
- Employee contribution (up to 10% of salary)
- Employer contribution (up to 14% for corporate)
- Tax benefits under Section 80CCD(1B)

### Sukanya Calculator
**Best for:** Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (girl child)

Features:
- Account for girls under 10 years
- Current rate: 8.2%
- Maturity at age 21

### NSC Calculator
**Best for:** National Savings Certificate

5-year lock-in with 7.7% interest. Tax deduction under 80C.

### SCSS Calculator
**Best for:** Senior Citizens Savings Scheme

For citizens 60+ years:
- Maximum deposit: ₹30 lakh
- Current rate: 8.2%
- Quarterly interest payout

## ArthaAI Chatbot

### What is ArthaAI?

ArthaAI is an AI-powered financial assistant powered by Llama 3.3 (70B parameters) via Groq, with deep knowledge of Indian financial products and regulations.

### How to Access

1. Click the **ArthaAI** button in the sidebar (top)
2. Or visit `/chat` directly

### Features

**Natural Language Queries:**
- "How much should I save for retirement?"
- "What's the difference between PPF and ELSS?"
- "Calculate EMI for ₹50 lakh home loan at 8.5%"
- "Which tax regime is better for ₹15 LPA salary?"

**Research-Powered Answers:**
ArthaAI has access to research on dynamic withdrawal strategies, so it can answer:
- "What are dynamic withdrawal strategies?"
- "How does the 4% rule work?"
- "What does research say about sustainable withdrawal rates?"

**Indian Context:**
- Always uses ₹ (Indian Rupees)
- References Indian products: PPF, NPS, ELSS, Nifty 50, Sensex
- Uses Indian tax slabs (Old/New Regime)
- References Indian platforms: Groww, Zerodha, Kuvera

**Chat History:**
- Your conversations are saved locally
- Access previous chats from the sidebar
- Clear chat history anytime

### Tips for Better Answers

1. **Be specific:** "₹50,000 monthly SIP for 20 years at 12%" instead of "How much will SIP give?"
2. **Mention context:** "I'm 30 years old, earning ₹15 LPA" helps get personalized advice
3. **Ask follow-ups:** "What if I increase by 10% annually?" or "Which fund is best for this?"

### Rate Limits

- 15 messages per minute
- If you hit the limit, wait a moment and try again

## Tips & Best Practices

### For Retirement Planning

1. **Start early** — Even ₹5,000/month from age 25 can grow to ₹5+ crore by 60
2. **Use stochastic calculator** — Don't rely only on fixed returns; check probability of success
3. **Account for inflation** — Always use 6-7% inflation, not 4-5%
4. **Review annually** — Recalculate as income and expenses change

### For Investment

1. **SIP over lumpsum** — Rupee cost averaging reduces timing risk
2. **Step-up SIP** — Increase 10% annually to match salary growth
3. **Diversify** — Don't put all money in one fund or asset class
4. **Stay invested** — Don't panic during market dips

### For Tax Planning

1. **Compare regimes** — Use the Old vs New Regime calculator every year
2. **Max out 80C** — ₹1.5 lakh in PPF/ELSS saves ₹46,800 in tax (30% bracket)
3. **Don't invest just for tax** — Good investments with tax benefits are better than bad investments with tax benefits

### For Loans

1. **Prepay high-interest loans first** — Personal loans (15%) before home loans (8.5%)
2. **Use prepayment calculator** — See how much you save by paying extra
3. **Compare offers** — Even 0.5% rate difference saves lakhs over 20 years

### For Insurance

1. **Term insurance first** — Pure protection is cheapest and most effective
2. **Adequate cover** — 10-15x annual income minimum
3. **Health insurance** — Don't rely only on employer coverage

## Troubleshooting

### Page not loading
- Check internet connection
- Clear browser cache
- Try a different browser

### Calculator shows unexpected results
- Verify all inputs are in correct units (years vs months, % vs decimal)
- Check if inputs are within valid ranges

### ArthaAI not responding
- Wait a moment (rate limit: 15 req/min)
- Refresh the page
- Check if Groq API is available

### Stock price not loading
- Verify the stock symbol is correct
- Some stocks may be delisted or suspended
- Try searching by company name instead of symbol
